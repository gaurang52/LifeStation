Request Account & Data Deletion – LifeStation

LifeStation (by LifeStation Inc., the developer of the LifeStation app on Google Play) lets you request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@allwellapp.com from the email address linked to your LifeStation account.
  2. Use the subject line: Request account deletion (or similar).
  3. Include your registered email address so we can identify your account.
  4. We will process your request and confirm once your account and data have been deleted.

What we delete

When you request account deletion, we delete or anonymize:

  • Your account and profile (name, email, password)
  • Your linked devices and device data
  • Event history and location data associated with your account
  • Care circle and caregiver links
  • Other data we hold that identifies you

What we may keep

We may retain data only where required by law (e.g. tax, legal hold) or for legitimate operational purposes (e.g. anonymized analytics). Such data is not linked to your identity after deletion.

How long it takes

We aim to complete account and data deletion within 30 days of your request. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.

For questions about your data or this process, contact us at support@allwellapp.com.
