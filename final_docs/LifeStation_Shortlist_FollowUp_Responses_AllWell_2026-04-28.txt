Responses to Code Ownership, Independence, and Architecture Questions
Prepared for: LifeStation
 Prepared by: AllWell

Executive Note
AllWell confirms a work-for-hire engagement with exclusive intellectual property ownership by LifeStation, fully aligned with the RFP.
In response to LifeStation’s requirement for a fully independent and deployable solution, we confirm:
A new, greenfield codebase will be created
Entire system will be owned, hosted, and controlled by LifeStation
No dependency on AllWell-hosted services, platforms, or internal systems
Delivered solution will be self-contained and production-ready within LifeStation’s environment
Prior experience and domain knowledge may inform implementation, but no runtime or architectural dependency will exist on AllWell systems.

 Yes.
